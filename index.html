Happy Birthday Shruti Mero Maya

Skipping away as her feet lifted off with each step
She tumbled on her knees, bleeding on the the empty street
It wasn’t the wound of the fall that stung at all
But the reflection of her broken self she saw
Her father, breathless and filled with anger
Only mourned the shattered phone which wouldn’t even linger
While he cradled the shattered soulless object more than his own daughter
It was the scars of betrayal which hurt the most that she shouldered

Years later, the lamp shines on her desperate grip of her pen
While the chirps of the outside crickets echoed her anger then
It wasn’t the sharp ache of the tireless studying that stings
Nor was it the sleepless nights that left her with bloodshot eyes
It was never the pain of enduring the quiet torture of the work
But the tears that soaked the letters of “justice” on her notebook

She bled every single day to prove her existence
To the same people who never saw her as the love felt distant
With no one on sight to hear out what her heart suppressed
She finally stopped waiting for a rescue to her grief she held in her chest

Standing before an unbroken mirror, she finally saw love from her own eyes
The hardwork and perseverance that was once used to cope with her despair,
Fuzed together with harmony to uplift her spirit with absolute care
Setting down the heavy expectations she shouldered for years
She slowly began to pick up her pen, not to gasp for the validation she tried to earn
But to write the melody of her rescue song, in the pages where her passions truly burn

And while the little girl may have been weeping on the dusty street
The woman she became, walked the road of resilience to make her complete.
Healing wasn’t gentle as she bled piecing her heart back together
Knowing that even during stormy days, the light would still pave through the harsh weather
She now reaches down to the child whose cries were left unheard in the past
Embracing her with the safety and hope, as she holds the kid’s hands with love at last
